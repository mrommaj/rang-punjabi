📖 Rang Rules & Strategy (ਰੰਗ ਦੇ ਨਿਯਮ)

🎴 Overview & Partnerships

Rang (also known as Court Piece or Coat Pees) is a classic 4-player trick-taking game played in fixed partnerships: South & North (You & Partner) vs West & East (Opponents).

🎺 The Deal & Calling Trump (ਰੰਗ)

1. Each player is dealt 5 cards in the first round.

2. The Trump Caller (Hukmi / Peera) inspects their 5 cards and selects the Trump Suit (♠️ Spades, ♥️ Hearts, ♦️ Diamonds, or ♣️ Clubs).

3. The remaining 8 cards are dealt (total 13 cards per player).

⚔️ Trick Play & Cutting (ਕੱਟਣਾ)

• The Trump Caller leads the first trick. Turn moves clockwise.

• Must Follow Suit: You must play a card of the led suit if you have one.

• Trump Cut (Katto): If you have NO cards of the led suit, you can play a Trump card to win the trick!

• Highest Trump wins; if no trump played, highest card of led suit wins (A > K > Q > J > 10... > 2).

🔥 Game Variations

• Single Sar (Classic): Tricks are collected immediately. First team to 7 tricks wins the hand.

• Double Sar: Won tricks accumulate in the center heap until one player wins 2 consecutive tricks in a row to sweep the whole stash!

• Hidden Rung (Band Rang): Trump card is placed face-down. Secret until a player revokes and opens the trump card!

👑 Kote & Bawla Kote Scoring

• Kote (7-0): Winning the first 7 tricks straight without letting opponents win any trick (2 Match Points)!

• Bawla Kote (13-0): Clean sweep of all 13 tricks in a hand (3 Match Points)!
