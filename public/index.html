📖 Rang Rules & Strategy (ਨਿਯਮ)

🎴 Partnerships

4 players in 2 fixed teams: You & Partner (South & North) vs Opponents (West & East).

🎺 Calling Trump (ਰੰਗ)

Each player is dealt 5 cards first. The Trump Caller inspects them and selects Trump (♠️ Spades, ♥️ Hearts, ♦️ Diamonds, ♣️ Clubs). Then remaining 8 cards are dealt (13 cards total).

⚔️ Cutting (ਕੱਟਣਾ)

• Must follow suit if you have cards of that suit.

• If out of led suit, play a Trump card to cut and win the trick!

🔥 Modes & Kote (ਕੋਟ)

• Single Sar: First to 7 tricks wins hand. Winning first 7 straight (7-0) is a Kote! 13-0 is a Bawla Kote.

• Double Sar: Win 2 tricks in a row to take all cards from center heap!

• Hidden Rung: Trump card is hidden face-down until someone revokes/cuts.
